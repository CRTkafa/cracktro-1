CRTkafa / Direct3D 11 demo

Run CRTkafa.exe. No companion assets or audio files are needed.
Windows 10/11 x64. Hardware feature level 11.0, or Windows WARP fallback.

F     Fullscreen / windowed (starts fullscreen)
ESC   Quit immediately
G     Cycle flash intensity: reduced / disabled / full

Losing focus or dragging/resizing pauses the picture and sound together.
The 2:54 sequence fades to silence and exits instead of replaying the intro.

This demo contains flashing lights and moving high-contrast patterns.
The offline flash check is not a medical safety certification.

Model attribution is in CREDITS.md; retain it when redistributing.

Developer verification used silent offline rendering and complete MP4 decoding.
Interactive window/audio playback was not launched automatically.
