# crtkafa, cracktro #1

Windows x64. One executable.

Download **CRTkafa.exe** directly; no ZIP or installation needed.

**F** switches fullscreen/windowed. **ESC** quits. **G** cycles flash intensity.
Windows 10/11 and Direct3D 11 required; software fallback may be slow.

**Flashing lights and moving high-contrast patterns.**

Also attached: **CRTkafa.nfo**, **RUNME.txt**, and **CREDITS.md**.
Keep the model credits when redistributing.
